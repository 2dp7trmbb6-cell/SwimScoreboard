04 / POOL ALIGNMENT

Fit the pool first. Fine-tune each lane.

Shows labeled placeholder cards even without a meet or timing source, overriding output visibility and race-start hiding for lane overlays. Exit alignment before going on air.

Place pool corners in order: start end / side A, finish end / side A, finish end / side B, start end / side B. Lane 1 begins at side A unless reversed. The app divides the pool into equal-width lanes in perspective. Drag the four handles below; click a lane polygon to fine-tune its card.

Reference image fills the OBS canvas; use a 1920 × 1080 screenshot of your scene. It stays in this browser and is never sent to OBS or saved. Fit all corners exposes off-screen handles.

Hold a direction button to repeat. Arrow keys on the alignment canvas nudge the selection; Shift moves 10 times faster. Individual adjustments remain as pixel offsets when the pool moves. Reset lane adjustment returns that card to the calculated pool position.

Existing manual lane positions are preserved until you enable pool geometry.
